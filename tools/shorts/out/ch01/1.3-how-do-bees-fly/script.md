## Fact check

- **About a million neurons.** A honeybee brain has roughly 960,000 neurons (Menzel and Giurfa, 2001); a human brain about 86 billion (Azevedo et al., 2009).
- **Centring.** Bees flying down a tunnel balance the image speed on their two sides (Srinivasan et al., 1991, the source credited on screen).
- **Landing.** Bees hold the ground's image angular speed roughly constant while descending, so forward speed falls in proportion to height (Srinivasan et al., 2000).
- **Minimal Perception.** The lab's framework, as described on praisecu.com.
